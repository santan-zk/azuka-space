Academic Planner

Your university work and your own engineering in one place. Saved in this browser only.

How to use this planner
  1. Add a task. Press New task, pick Academic or Engineering, and add a deadline and an estimate if you have them. Engineering tasks do not need a course.
  2. Add courses (optional). Press New course so academic tasks can be linked to one.
  3. Track your time. Press Start timer on a task, or Log time to add minutes by hand. Your estimate and your logged time are kept apart.
  4. Finish tasks. Mark complete when done. A task is overdue only while it is open and its deadline has passed.
  5. Set your split. Open Workload split below to choose your planned hours and your engineering share (50% to 70%). It is a guide, and deadlines always show at the top.
  6. See your habits. Open Activity below for a heat graph of the days you logged time.
  7. Back up. Your data lives only in this browser. Open Your data and use Export now and then.
