Experiment Not a project, a learning tool

Sorting visualiser

Bubble sort, one step at a time. It compares neighbouring values and swaps them when the left one is bigger, and repeats until a full pass needs no swaps. Every step on screen is a recorded step of the real algorithm.

Autoplay is off because your device asks for reduced motion. Use Step or Jump to end.

  • Not touched this step
  • Being compared
  • Just swapped
  • In its final place

In big-O terms: about n²/2 comparisons on average and in the worst case, so O(n²). With the early stop used here the best case, a list that is already sorted, is O(n). It needs O(1) extra memory. It is a teaching algorithm, not what you would use for large data, and this page is not a benchmark.
